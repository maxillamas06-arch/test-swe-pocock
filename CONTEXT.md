# Manos Regias

Content and domain vocabulary for the Manos Regias website: a 3-page static site for a new food-security volunteer nonprofit in Monterrey, Mexico.

## Language

**Beneficiario**:
A person or family the program serves — specifically, someone facing food insecurity in Monterrey who receives support through a Comedor comunitario.
_Avoid_: "Los necesitados," "the needy" — too vague to write consistent copy against.

**Donante**:
A person who gives money to the program, via bank transfer (CLABE).
_Avoid_: Conflating with Voluntario — a Donante gives money, not time.

**Voluntario**:
A person who gives time to the program by signing up through the site's volunteer form.
_Avoid_: Conflating with Donante — a Voluntario gives time, not money.

**Comedor comunitario**:
A community kitchen / food distribution point — the physical mechanism through which the program delivers food security support to Beneficiarios.
